# Geographic data attribution

The bundled topology was prepared from [nvkelso/natural-earth-vector](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector) at commit [`ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19`](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/commit/ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19). Natural Earth data is public domain.

- **Moving globe context:** [`ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson`](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson), stored as `world-countries-low.topojson`. This adaptive layer is used only during drag, zoom and transition frames.
- **Global country context at rest:** [`ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson`](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson), stored as `world-countries-context.topojson`. It replaces the moving layer when interaction settles and is also used for the selected country during motion.
- **Detailed country geometry:** [`ne_10m_admin_0_countries.geojson`](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19/geojson/ne_10m_admin_0_countries.geojson), stored as `world-countries.topojson`. The selected country is rendered from this 1:10m geometry. Natural Earth's interior label coordinates are retained so labels do not drift to offshore geometric centroids.
- **Urban footprints:** [`ne_10m_urban_areas.geojson`](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19/geojson/ne_10m_urban_areas.geojson), stored in `world-cities.topojson`. These are built-up urban extents, **not administrative city limits**.
- **City names and reference points:** [`ne_10m_populated_places_simple.geojson`](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19/geojson/ne_10m_populated_places_simple.geojson). Country capitals and places with a source population of at least 500,000 are matched spatially to urban polygons. Aliases are retained for news-location matching. A small number of places without a matching source polygon remain explicitly represented as reference points.

Geometry is quantized and delta-encoded as TopoJSON to keep the offline bundle compact. Only editorial names and the fields needed by the map are retained. The MENA picker is an editorial coverage shortcut, not a declaration of sovereignty. Natural Earth boundaries and urban extents are generalized; contested areas can differ by source. This data is for geographic context and news visualization, not legal, administrative, or surveying reference.
